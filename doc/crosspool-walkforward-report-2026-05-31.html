Walk-Forward Gap Analysis

Why the gap is near zero

1. h=opposing is a structural filter, not a data-mined one
The filter was validated across CN and US pools independently before being applied to the combined portfolio. Its cross-market universality suggests genuine economic rationale (momentum continuation vs. counter-trend exhaustion).
2. No parameter tuning on the portfolio level
The equal-weight 1R/signal allocation was chosen on first principles, not optimized against IS returns. No pool weights were fitted to historical data.
3. Cross-pool diversification reduces IS luck
With 5 uncorrelated pools, any single lucky regime in IS is diluted. The IS result is already a diversified average, making it a better predictor of future performance than a single-pool IS result would be.
