Why CN Commodity Exhaustion Reversals Failed Against 1h Trend in 2024

China Property Collapse

The property sector (20-30% of GDP) collapsed in 2024. Steel demand fell sharply → rebar (RB) and iron ore prices broke structural support levels. Normal exhaustion patterns where "counter-trend 1h opposition predicts reversal" failed because the trend itself was driven by a structural fundamental shift, not momentum excess.

Persistent Downtrend Overrides Exhaustion

h=opposing signals in a structural bear market (where 1h trend is down, opposing = 1h up) typically work well — they catch oversold bounces. But in 2024, CN commodities experienced trending-down moves with no sustained bounce, so h=opposing bottom signals that expected a reversal kept hitting stops instead of TP2.

Regime Predictor, Not Filter Failure

The 2024 episode reinforces the earlier finding (2026-05 exhaustion detector WF): individual feature axes (h=opposing, confidence, wick) are regime predictors, not static filters. The filter works when macro conditions support reversal; it fails when structural trends overwhelm technical exhaustion signals. Do not add a "2024 exclusion" rule — that would be overfitting.

What Is Actually Happening Post Rate-Cut

1. Signal frequency collapses

In a post-cut bull market, the 1h trend is predominantly upward (supporting = same direction as bottom signal). h=opposing requires 1h to be bearish while daily is exhausted low. Bull markets produce few such setups. US_EQUITY fires 0-1 h=opposing signals per quarter post-cut vs 2-4 pre-cut.

2. When signals do fire, filter still works sometimes

2025Q2: n=3 opp, EV +1.500R (lift +0.625R). The filter is not broken — signals are just rare. The +0.375R post-cut average includes 2025Q4 n=2 EV=−1.000R (full stop on both), which dominates given small n.

3. Bull market = lower reversal quality when it does oppose

In a sustained bull market, any dip that generates a bearish 1h signal (h=opposing for a bottom signal) is often quickly reversed upward — but the underlying was already in a strong uptrend, so the "exhaustion reversal" has a shorter bounce duration than in bear/volatile markets.

Verdict: US_EQUITY post-cut weakness is real (+0.375R vs +1.105R) but the mechanism is primarily signal scarcity + small-n noise, not a structural breakdown of the filter. The filter lift remains positive in years where signals occur at sufficient frequency (2022-2025). Monitor post-cut n — if it stays below 2/quarter, the strategy simply has lower throughput in bull markets.
