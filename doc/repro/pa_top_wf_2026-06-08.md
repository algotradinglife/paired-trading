# PA TOP multi-dimensional walk-forward validation — 2026-06-08

K=3 walk-forward grid validation of `PATopDetector` against four pools
to inform the still-placeholder `PATopDetector.policy_weight()`
(currently returns 0.0 for every input).  The headline result is that
**the placeholder weights are vindicated** — no cell across any pool
clears the promotion bar (n_oos ≥ 20, EV_oos > 0, sign stable across
OOS1 / OOS2 / OOS3) under the standard configuration tested.

## Run parameters

- Detector: `PATopDetector(min_l_legs=2, min_quality=0.3, ema_threshold=0.0, min_gap=10)`
- Simulation: short mirror of `simulate_trade` in `backtest_pa_swing.py` —
  `stop = entry + 1.5×ATR`, `TP1 = entry − 1R` (trail to BE), `TP2 = entry − 2R`,
  max_hold = 40, ATR period = 14.  R definitions match the BOTTOM detector.
- Folds: IS ≤ 2022-12-31, OOS1 ≤ 2023-12-31, OOS2 ≤ 2024-12-31, OOS3 > 2025-01-01
- Top divergence helper (`top_div`, in-script — does NOT add APIs to `pa_detector`):
  `hist[idx] < hist[idx-5]` AND (`hist[idx-5] > 0` OR `hist[idx] < 0`).
- Grid keys: `phase × h_rel × context × top_div`.
- Promotion gate: n_oos ≥ 20, EV_oos > 0, OOS fold signs all equal.

Script: `src/scripts/backtest_pa_top_grid.py`.  Output: `data/review/pa_top_wf_grid.csv`.

## Promotion verdict

Under the promotion gate (n_oos ≥ 20, EV_oos > 0, OOS folds all
same sign), **no cell promotes**.

The closest contender, `cn_commodity_daily × TR_FORMING × h=opposing ×
top_div=True`, n = 20, EV = +0.073 R, fails the stability test
(OOS1 = −0.25, OOS2 = +0.43, OOS3 = −0.005 — sign flips both ways).
Excluding the divergence filter gives n = 63, EV = +0.012 R, also
sign-flipping (F1 = −0.05, F2 = −0.01, F3 = +0.05) — i.e. the lift
is concentrated in 2025-onward bars and barely above noise.

## Does CN SHORT emphasis bear out?

Mixed evidence, leaning **no** under this configuration:

- `cn_commodity_daily` is the only pool with a positive pool-level
  h=opp EV (+0.043 R, n = 97).  This very weakly supports the
  "短更易" hypothesis but the lift is one fold's worth of noise away
  from zero.
- `cn_metal_daily` h=opp is −0.153 R (n = 54) and `cn_bond_daily`
  h=opp is −0.275 R (n = 29).  Both CN-domestic pools that the user
  highlighted look worse than US in absolute EV terms.
- `us_60min` h=opp is −0.031 R (n = 1 664) — basically flat.

The MVP-level conclusion: the TOP lane is **not** the asymmetric edge
the user expected on CN; if anything CN daily tops fade harder than US
60min tops, and CN commodity daily is the only daily slice with a
non-negative EV — and only marginally so.

## Caveats and data-coverage issues

1. **CN bond 60min coverage starts 2021-05-06** while CN bond daily
   goes back to 2018-01-02.  ~3.5 years of CN bond daily signals
   therefore have `h_rel = None` (and were not in the OOS folds anyway).
   Bond OOS sample is genuinely thin (n_oos = 43 with any h_rel).
2. **h_bars for CN daily pools is 60min, not weekly** — this is a
   cross-TF DIF anchor rather than a true "higher TF" reference.  The
   task brief explicitly directed this configuration.  Replacing with
   a weekly h_bars when weekly data lands may shift the cn_metal /
   cn_commodity h=opp rows.
3. **Context classifier is daily-only**, by design.  us_60min cells
   never carry a context label.  This bakes in a structural difference
   between the daily and 60min rollups: CN pools have 1 extra dim
   the US pool doesn't.  In practice almost no CN signal carried
   `context = "A"` because A requires DIF > 0 and PA tops on daily
   typically fire on DIF ≤ 0 setups.  Only `B1` shows up (a handful
   of cells, n ≤ 8).
4. **min_quality = 0.3 and min_gap = 10 mirror the daily BOTTOM
   detector**.  Looser params on US 60min would push the fire count
   higher but probably wouldn't change cell EV signs (TR_FORMING is
   already saturated).
5. **No 15min confirmation** is layered on top.  Per the strategic
   decision locked the same day, 15min confirmation is required for
   CN_METAL only and is annotation-only elsewhere; adding it as a
   filter is the next experiment if any cell were close to promoting.

## Recommendation

- **Keep `PATopDetector.policy_weight()` at 0.0 across all
  instrument_classes.**  No grid cell meets the promotion gate, and
  the cells with the most consistent OOS direction are
  consistently *negative* (us_60min TR_FORMING × opp × div=T,
  cn_bond TR_FORMING × opp).
- **Do not turn the TOP lane on for the Xiao MVP under the
  CN-short-emphasis assumption.**  CN-domestic tops are weaker in
  this validation, not stronger, than US tops.
- Inconclusive cells (50 of 62 rows in the CSV) have n_oos < 20.
  Many are interesting but cannot drive a weight under this validation
  budget.  Notably `cn_metal_daily BEAR × h=opp × div=False` (n=1,
  R = +0.94) and `cn_bond_daily BULL × h=opp × div=False` (n_oos=3,
  EV = +0.67 R) — sample sizes too small to act on.
