2. **Selling rallies in TR_FORMING has no edge** — the dominant cell is negative

3. **BEAR-phase A_top is too rare to validate** (US n=15, CN n=2) — even if its
   tiny-n EV looks non-negative, there is no sample base to promote.
4. **Aggregate (not just folds) is negative**, so alternate cutoff framings can't
   rescue it — re-slicing fold boundaries doesn't change a negative total EV.
