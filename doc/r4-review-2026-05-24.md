## Methodology

Six checks applied per key finding:

| # | Check | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **HHI** | Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (0–1) — symbol concentration per bucket. HHI > 0.25 indicates moderate concentration; > 0.5 is high. |
| 2 | **Bootstrap 95% CI** | 1,000 resamples with replacement. CI that excludes zero = statistically distinguishable from zero at α=0.05 (uncorrected). |
| 3 | **Drop-top-2** | Drop the two largest returns and recompute mean. Flags outlier-driven findings. |
| 4 | **Bonferroni** | α = 0.05 / 34 buckets tested across both reports ≈ 0.0015. |
| 5 | **Direction split** | Mixed-direction buckets disaggregated. |
| 6 | **Year/regime breakdown** | Per-year mean + 95% CI for 2021–2026. |

## SECTION IV: STATISTICAL RED FLAGS

### 4.1 Lookahead / data-snooping concerns
- **Window overlap with known regimes**: The 2024-2026 B-topology window covers the Q4 2024 CN equity rally + 2025 gold rally. These are the dominant drivers of the F8 and bottom-side results. The 21y baseline's +0.65% top mean is suppressed in this window.
- **No out-of-sample validation**: All 233 signals are within the same 2.4y window. No cross-validation split.
- **Multi-TF enrichment is in-sample**: The higher-TF (1h) and lower-TF (15m) relations are computed on the same window as the forward returns.

### 4.2 Small-sample issues
| Bucket | n | Issue |
|--------|---|-------|
| top+higher_opposing | 20 | CI includes zero. Proposed B1 rule rider with weight 1.20 needs n≥50. |
| bottom+higher_neutral | 10 | +8.71% but 90% hit — extreme small-n noise. |
| F8 forming band | 7 | +8.07% → +0.52% after drop-top-2. Entirely 2 trades. |
| F8 watching band | 7 | +1.65% → +0.12% after drop-top-2. |
| top+higher_neutral | 9 | -4.65% → -7.22% after drop-top. Gets worse, not better. |

### 4.3 Outlier sensitivity — critical cells
| Bucket | Original | Drop-top-2 | Collapse? |
|--------|----------|------------|-----------|
| Options bottom | +35.1% | +3.3% | **YES** — entirely 2 big winners |
| Options "no rule" | +119.5% | +6.0% | **YES** — 2 outliers |
| F8 forming band | +8.07% | +0.52% | **YES** — 2 outliers |
| Options au F8 | +118.6% | +13.5% | **YES** — 2 outliers |
| B-top bottom | +2.58% | +1.94% | No — robust |
| B-top top | -1.06% | -1.35% | No — robust, gets worse |
| B-top F8 | +3.81% | +2.33% | No — robust |

### 4.4 Year-regime dependence
- **F8 options**: negative in 2023-2024, positive in 2025. This is a regime story (gold rally), not a stable signal.
- **Bottom options**: negative in 2024 (-42.5%), the year with most CN commodity weakness.
- **F8 B-topology**: positive every year from 2024-2026, and strengthening.
- **Top B-topology**: negative every year from 2024-2026, strengthening.

### 4.5 Bonferroni picture
- At Bonferroni-corrected α = 0.0015, only **F8 (bottom+weakness)** survives for B-topology.
- No option-payoff finding survives Bonferroni (all CIs wide).
- The correction is conservative (34 buckets), but the message is clear: F8 is the only individually significant bucket.

## SECTION V: RECOMMENDATIONS

### Policy changes that survive R4 scrutiny
1. **Reaffirm `F8-cn-no-boost` (weight 1.0)** — F8 is the most robust finding in both B-topology and options.
2. **Adopt `top+higher_supporting` de-weight (weight 0.80)** — n=74, CI near-excludes zero, consistent across years. **This is the highest-confidence policy addition.**
3. **Keep `CN1-top-passthrough` (weight 1.0)** — top sign-flip confirmed but variance is high. No evidence that a uniform de-weight helps.
4. **Keep symbol blacklist (j/jm)** — coal bottom failure confirmed directionally.

### Policy changes that FAIL R4 scrutiny (need more data)
5. **DO NOT adopt CN-B1 rule (top+higher_opposing, weight 1.20)** — n=20, CI includes zero. Monitor at n≥50.
6. **DO NOT adopt F8 low-band bump** — forming band collapses on outlier removal.
7. **DO NOT act on "no rule" bottom cell** — HHI 0.820, m-dominated, collapses on drop-top-2.

### Operational notes
8. **Tight stops on CN1-top-passthrough options are essential** — raw -42.6% becomes +11.1% at SL -10%.
9. **F8 on options needs product-specific thresholds** — bimodality pattern is plausible but unconfirmed. Gold-only F8 bot options had +118.6% (even if CI wide); iron ore/copper F8 was negative.
